9:45 PM

No frontier model required.

Every step today ran on a model on Sam's Mac, apart from the one question Sam chose to send out. Small models get written recipes to follow, so the floor is local. Bigger brains are an option, never a requirement.

Yours to shape.

Open core. The core is open source. Copy it, change it, label it as your own. The True Convo name is the only thing kept back.
Your keys or none. Runs on local models, or on your own sign-ins and keys. Cloud costs pass straight through.
Rename anything. Modules, spaces, the assistant itself. Move them, hide them, change nothing underneath.
Leave any time. Export everything in a form that works without us. Restore brings back your information, never permission to act.
Switch the AI off. Lists, calendar, notes and export keep working with no model at all.
A floor no edition can remove. Approval before anything leaves. Nothing deleted silently. The microphone never surprises you.
